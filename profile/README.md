## Spalla Limited

Spalla Limited is a Nairobi company building **Spalla Finance**, a group savings and personal finance
platform for Kenya.

With Spalla Finance, members can:

- save together in **chamas**, with contributions, group payouts and group loans;
- set **personal saving goals** and track their progress;
- **invest** in products offered by Spalla Limited;
- pay in and withdraw with **M-Pesa**, from the web or an app installed on their phone.

 This is where the people who build Spalla Finance work together.
